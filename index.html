Who we are

Mwangaza means light —
and light is what we offer.

Mwangaza Children's Centre is a registered children's home serving vulnerable children and families in Baringo County, Kenya. We walk alongside children who have lost the safety of family — offering shelter, schooling, food, and the kind of steady presence that lets a child imagine a future again.

Our Mission

To restore hope to vulnerable children through shelter, education, nutrition, and holistic care that prepares them for independent, dignified lives.

Our Vision

A Baringo where every child grows up safe, educated, and known — with the chance to become whoever they were meant to be.

01

Compassion

Every child who comes to us is met first as a person, not a case file — with patience, warmth, and time.

02

Perseverance

Rebuilding a childhood takes years, not weeks. We stay — through school terms, setbacks, and small wins.

03

Belonging

We build family where family was lost, so every child grows up knowing they are wanted and known.

What we do

Care that covers the whole child

Shelter & Care

A safe, family-style home with house-mothers, clean beds, and daily routines that give children back a sense of stability.

Education Support

School fees, uniforms, books, and tutoring — because an education is the surest way out of vulnerability.

Nutrition

Three balanced meals a day, prepared on-site, so children can concentrate on being children — and on school.

Health & Wellbeing

Routine medical check-ups, counselling, and play therapy that address both the body and what the heart has carried.

Sponsor Testimonials

Stories from sponsors about what supporting a child at Mwangaza has meant to them.

"We learned about Mwangaza Children's Centre through our Rotary Club chapter in South Florida. We helped raise funds to build a wall around their campus to provide the children with much-needed security. It turns out that the wall was completed just prior to the election riots breaking out. Because of that wall, Mwangaza Children's Centre was not only able to protect its own children, but was also able to take in many more children from around the country to keep them safe and sheltered throughout the chaos. We are so grateful that our small contribution not only helped the children of Mwangaza Children's Centre, but literally helped save the lives of so many others from around Kenya."

— Benjamin Cheboi Governor, Baringo County, Since 2024

"If you have ever visited East Africa, you would see the extraordinary level of poverty and, most heartbreakingly, the high number of children living and working on the streets. When you walk by them, you want to give them money so they can eat, but that only helps them for that day. Mwangaza Children's Centre gives us a chance to make a lasting impact every single day, and we are so happy to have found them. The child we sponsor was once living on the streets of his small village and struggling with substance abuse. Today, he is enrolling in college! We are so proud and so grateful to Mwangaza Children's Centre for supporting his rehabilitation and education, both in and out of the classroom."

— Kim Parsons, since 2023
